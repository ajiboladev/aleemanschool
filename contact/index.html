FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

What are your admission requirements?

Requirements vary by grade level. Generally, you'll need previous school certificates, birth certificate, passport photographs, and medical certificate. Visit our Admissions page for detailed requirements.

When does the academic year start?

The academic year typically begins in September. Our academic calendar is divided into three terms. Check our Academic Calendar for specific dates.

Do you offer scholarships?

Yes! We offer scholarships based on academic excellence, Qur'an memorization, and financial need. Scholarship applications can be submitted with your admission application.

What is your student-teacher ratio?

We maintain a low student-teacher ratio of approximately 15:1 to ensure personalized attention and quality education for every student.

Do you have boarding facilities?

We offer both day and boarding options for students. Our boarding facilities are secure, comfortable, and supervised by qualified staff.

How can I pay school fees?

We accept payments via bank transfer, online payment, or direct payment at our accounts office. Payment plans are available for parents who need them.

Still have questions? Contact us directly and we'll be happy to help!
