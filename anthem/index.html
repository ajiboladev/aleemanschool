About the Anthem

This anthem beautifully encapsulates the essence of Aleeman School, celebrating the journey from ignorance to enlightenment through faith, knowledge, and dedication to Islamic learning.

Educational Philosophy

The anthem reflects our commitment to holistic education that combines Quranic memorization, Islamic jurisprudence, Arabic language mastery, and scientific knowledge.

Spiritual Foundation

Every verse emphasizes the spiritual transformation that occurs within these walls, where students develop both intellectual and moral excellence.
